# Imperial ship refresh — generation provenance

Generated with the built-in image generation tool; normalized and alpha-cleaned with
`tools/art/prepare_empire_sprites.py` under explicit user authorization.
The first schematic-corvette edits and the Union-reference recolor were rejected.
Final nine sources were generated independently, without reference images.
The shared wording mentions the Union as a quality benchmark; no Union pixels are used.
Source raster aspect ratios were normalized to existing engineering hull L/W.
Final PNGs in the production tree are the durable accepted artwork.

## Shared brief

Create ONE professional highly detailed 2D hard-science-fiction game spaceship sprite. Strict TOP DOWN ORTHOGRAPHIC, no perspective, nose exactly RIGHT, engines LEFT, entire ship centered. Isolate on perfectly flat pure white #FFFFFF background for later chroma extraction, no checkerboard, no shadow or glow outside hull. Landscape canvas. Ship occupies 85% width, narrow axial silhouette. Match the supplied Industrial Union reference ONLY for production quality: intricate convincing engineered surfaces, readable major components, fine fasteners and service seams, material texture, restrained wear and sharp rendering. DO NOT copy its hull. IMPERIAL faction distinct visual DNA: armored prow, especially strong layered CENTRAL CITADEL, segmented protected armor belts, maintenance hatches, recessed radiators, redundant conduits, compact sensors and RCS. 60% graphite gunmetal #41494E #1F2930, 25% warm ivory-grey #D1CCC0 armor, 8% dark burgundy #6D2933 faction panels, less than 3% old brass heraldic accents; tiny muted cyan instrument windows. Old disciplined monarchic navy, maintained and modernized, quiet institutional power. No historical literal warships, fantasy towers/wings, gold covering, steampunk, random greebles, text, label, border, stars, exhaust or VFX. Clear large shapes AND fine details, as crisp and richly engineered as the reference. All hardware stowed inside compact silhouette.

## Final constraints

Continuous armored central hull/citadel for military families; no disconnected Union
block-spine. Civilian families emphasize cargo, tanks and stowed workshop equipment.
Only the corvette excludes additional gun turrets. Generated decorative details are
presentation and do not introduce installed equipment or override engineering mounts.
